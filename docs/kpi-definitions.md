# KPI Definitions

Definitions follow the requirements document. Page references match the dashboard navigation.

## Executive Summary
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Total value of completed sales |
| Net Revenue | Revenue after returns, refunds and discounts |
| Net Profit | Profit after cost of goods and other deductions |
| Net Margin % | Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue |
| Marketing ROI % | Return generated relative to marketing spend |
| Return Rate % | Share of items returned |

## Product Discovery
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Sessions / Unique Users | Overall site activity |
| Bounce Rate % | Share of single-page sessions |
| Search Success Rate % | Share of searches that lead to a product page view |
| Returning Visitor Rate % | Share of visitors who have visited before |
| Zero-Result Searches | Queries that returned no products (missing-product / search-quality signal) |

## Funnel & Cart Analysis
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Add-to-Cart Rate % | Products added to cart ÷ product views |
| Cart Abandonment Rate % | Initiated carts that do not become a completed order |
| Avg Cart Value | Average monetary value of items in a cart |
| Upsell / Cross-sell Rate % | Effectiveness of recommended products |
| Purchase-to-Click Ratio | Product-level link between clicks/views and purchases |

## Checkout & Transactions
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| AOV | Average revenue per order |
| Conversion Rate | Share of sessions that convert to an order |
| Promotion Redemption Rate | Promotions used ÷ promotions offered |
| Discount Impact | Revenue and orders influenced by each promotion |
| Payment Success vs Failure | Successful vs failed payment attempts over time, by method |

## Fulfillment & Shipping
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Avg Days to Deliver | Average time from order to delivery |
| On-Time Delivery % | Orders delivered within the estimated timeframe |
| Avg Shipping Cost | Average shipping cost per order |
| Total Delayed Orders | Orders delivered after the estimated date |
| Delivery Failure | Shipments recorded as failed, by carrier |

## Post-Purchase
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| NPS | Net Promoter Score: likelihood customers recommend the business |
| Avg Product Rating | Average star rating across products |
| Repeat Customer Rate % | Customers with more than one purchase |
| Churn Rate % | Share of customers who stopped purchasing |
| Total Refund Amount | Value refunded on returns |
| Return Reasons | Breakdown of returns by reason code |
| At-Risk Products | Products ranked by share of negative reviews |
